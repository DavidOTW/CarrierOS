Trust before your first upload

Understand what CarrierOS protects, stores, and still needs you to configure.

Small carriers should be able to ask direct questions about their operational data. This page describes the current application controls without pretending that a hosting setting or a checklist replaces an independent security review.

Current controls

Designed for private carrier workspaces.

01

Workspace isolation

Each signup receives an organization-scoped workspace. Authenticated records, loads, payments, documents, and exports are filtered to that organization.

02

Stripe-hosted card handling

CarrierOS sends subscription checkout and customer-portal payment details to Stripe. The application does not ask for or store card or bank credentials.

03

Audit and export paths

Important changes are recorded in the workspace audit trail, and administrators can download company data plus filtered load CSV exports.

04

Private document boundaries

RateCon and delivery-document workflows require configured private storage and malware scanning. Audit uploads are processed for structured findings and raw files are not retained.

Operator responsibilities

Production safety still depends on the deployment.

Backups: configure daily logical backups, off-host copies, and a tested restore process.

Secrets: use host-managed environment variables for session, Stripe, SMTP, storage, and scanner credentials.

Access: use strong passwords, least-privilege staff accounts, and a verified support mailbox.

Review: have qualified legal and security professionals review the policies and sensitive-data workflow before a high-volume launch.

Do not upload Social Security numbers, bank credentials, full account numbers, tax IDs, or identity-document images. CarrierOS is an operations and profitability workspace, not a payroll, accounting, legal, tax, insurance, or regulatory system.
